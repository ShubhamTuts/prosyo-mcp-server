Architecture

One hop. No local process.

Your AI client speaks Streamable HTTP to a single public endpoint. Prosyo runs LinkedIn outreach, cold email sequences and the unified inbox behind it. There is no npm package, no Docker image and no daemon on your machine.

Use cases

What people actually run through it

Each of these is one prompt in the chat window. The AI picks the tools, shows you the plan and the credit cost, then waits for a yes before anything leaves your account.

01 — TRIGGER-BASED PROSPECTING

Build a list from a buying signal, not a job title

Pull companies showing a trigger, score them against your ICP, keep the ones worth a message and drop the rest before a single credit is spent on research.

“Find agencies hiring SDRs this month, score them against my ICP, save the top 50 to a list.”

02 — PERSONALISATION AT VOLUME

One real observation per prospect

Research returns sourced facts, not adjectives, and turns them into an icebreaker, a connection note and a first email you can read before anything sends.

“Research these 50, write me a first touch that opens with something specific to each company.”

03 — MULTICHANNEL SEQUENCES

LinkedIn and email in one cadence

Draft the full sequence, preview it merged against a real prospect, then launch it across LinkedIn outreach and cold email from the same chat.

“Build a 4-step sequence: connect, observation on day 3, Loom on day 7, break-up on day 12.”

04 — INBOX TRIAGE

Every reply, one place, drafted

Read LinkedIn and email replies in a single thread view, classify what's warm and get drafted responses you approve one by one.

“Sweep my unread inbox, tell me who's interested, and draft replies for the warm ones.”

05 — INTENT MONITORING

People in-market who never saw your campaign

Buying signals surface ICP-matched people from outside your own lists, with the source and date behind every signal so you can judge the “why now”.

“Who showed high intent this week? Show me the signals and save the unlocked ones to a list.”

06 — PIPELINE REPORTING

Ask your numbers instead of exporting them

Campaign health, reply rates, credits consumed and cost per reply, read straight out of the workspace with no CSV in the loop.

“Which campaign has the best reply rate, and what did each reply cost me in credits?”

Campaigns

Recent LinkedIn and email conversations. Optional search and unread filter.

draft_replyWrite

Draft an inbox reply. Returns text only. Sending uses send_inbox_reply.

send_inbox_replyWrite

Prepare a LinkedIn or email reply. The message sends after confirm_action.

Intent intelligence

get_intent_summaryRead

Tracking status, included people used and left, today's deliveries, High-intent and Warm counts, and the review queue.

list_intent_leadsRead

People or companies showing buying signals, strongest first, with score, fit and why now. Locked people show signals only.

get_intent_leadRead

One Intent person or company: every signal with source and date, fit reasons, research status and personalised lines.

get_ideal_customerRead

Ideal customer rules: roles, industries, company size, locations, exclusions and delivery settings.

list_playbooksRead

Playbooks and Intent-led audiences: signals watched, Review first or Autopilot, matches, people added, credits used.

People a playbook matched who are waiting for approval, or whose researched message is ready to review.

Signals by source, people delivered, researched, contacted, replied, meetings, customers and credits per reply.

Save unlocked Intent people to a list, with personalised lines. Free. Locked people are skipped. Idempotent.

Execution and safety

approve_reviewWrite

Prepare approving people in the review queue. Approval researches them for credits. The message is still visible before send.

confirm_actionWrite

Run a launch, enroll, resume, inbox send, unlock, research, enrichment or review approval after an explicit yes in chat. Pass the confirmation token from the previous tool.

The Model Context Protocol Registry is the community index of public MCP servers, run by the Model Context Protocol organization and backed by Anthropic, GitHub, Microsoft and PulseMCP. The service is modelcontextprotocol/registry; reference servers live in modelcontextprotocol/servers. Clients and downstream registries read that upstream metadata.

FAQ

Questions people ask first

Do I need an API key?

No. The endpoint takes no API key and no authentication headers. The first connection opens a browser allow screen once so you can choose a workspace, and that choice stays out of your config file.

Which AI clients work with it?

Claude Desktop, Cursor, Windsurf, VS Code with GitHub Copilot, and any other client that supports remote Streamable HTTP MCP servers. Configs for each are in the quickstart.

Can the AI message people on its own?

No. Every tool that contacts a person or spends credits prepares the action and returns a confirmation token. Nothing leaves your account until confirm_action runs after an explicit yes in the chat.

What is the Model Context Protocol?

MCP is an open standard that lets AI assistants call external tools and data directly, instead of a person copying data between apps. It is the difference between an AI that talks about your pipeline and one that can work it.

Is anything installed locally?

No. The server is hosted. You add one URL. There is no npm package, no Docker image and no background process on your machine.

Do I need a Prosyo account?

Yes — the tools act on your own workspace. You can start with Prosyo here, then connect the MCP server to it.
